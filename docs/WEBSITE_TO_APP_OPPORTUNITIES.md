## Ecosystem decisions

- Luxe Jetter + ShopMy: native ESCVDO and Barrio Italia articles use the existing destination wardrobe and collection components. Existing imagery/inventory and affiliate URLs remain intact.
- Let Them Eat: Nanai Cookies provides a specific Cookies-world connection in the Santiago story, using the already supported receiver. No invented food-world URLs.
- Rallii: the PeruRail article retains its published Rallii Rail App Store link. No matching route deep link is verified; no Cusco or Mendoza route is added to the app map. The Maipú article’s generic rail promotion is omitted from native reading.
- Little Jetter: existing family-intent and supported-city checks retained. The five selected stories do not justify new family CTAs.
- Jardins: no stops, coordinates or routes imported without the approved master record. Santiago’s three store relationships were checked directly against official store/gallery pages; no measured route was added to the planner.

## Accuracy and implementation boundaries

Twenty existing-place relationships were reviewed; incidental skyline views and museum artifacts were rejected as visit recommendations. Nearby suggestions use exact existing neighborhood names, are explicitly separate from article recommendations, and do not promise walking proximity. Original article text remains dated editorial; operational advice should be checked at source. Article body links keep affiliate tracking. All old IDs, saved content schema and starter itinerary data remain unchanged.
